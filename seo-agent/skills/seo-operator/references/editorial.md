# Editorial and evidence rules

- Write for the configured audience and search intent, not for keyword density.
- Treat metrics as observations, never forecasts or proof of causation.
- Record source URL, access time, supported point and a short evidence excerpt for factual claims.
- Do not invent testimonials, rankings, revenue, customer counts or product capabilities.
- Keep comparisons fair and date-sensitive claims freshly verified.
- Require human review for finance, legal, healthcare and other high-stakes content.
- Preserve useful nuance; remove filler only when meaning and sentence continuity remain intact.
